# Candidate LAMCORE–Microenvironment Associations

## Class: exploratory hypothesis

## Observation
LAMCORE-like candidates show computable expression associations with lymphatic endothelial, vascular endothelial, fibroblast and immune programs. These are co-variation signals, not evidence of physical ligand–receptor communication.

## Validation
Use spatial transcriptomics or imaging to establish proximity, then protein or functional assays for specific ligand–receptor, ECM or cytokine/growth-factor relationships.

## Novelty / confidence / priority
Novelty: medium; confidence: low-to-medium; priority: medium.
