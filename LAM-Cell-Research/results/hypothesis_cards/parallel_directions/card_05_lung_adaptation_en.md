# Hypothesis Card 05: acquired lung-adaptation program in LAMCORE

## Observation

In conservative LAMCORE candidate cells from LAM1–LAM4, the lung-adaptation program was approximately 0.667 higher than the donor-level median in the normal-lung reference and also 0.667 higher than the mouse-uterus lineage reference. The smooth-muscle lineage program was retained and was about 0.367 higher than the mouse-uterus reference, while the ECM-interaction program was about 1.216 lower.

## Preliminary interpretation

The pattern is compatible with a model in which LAMCORE retains smooth-muscle/lineage features while acquiring a partial lung-adaptation and ECM-interaction state. It suggests that pulmonary LAM may not simply reproduce a uterine lineage program, but recombine lineage features with lung-context programs.

## Important limitations

Normal lung is a multi-donor reference, whereas mouse uterus is one reference object. The cross-species comparison used conservative gene-symbol overlap rather than a complete formal ortholog map. These comparisons do not prove cell origin, migration direction or causal adaptation.

## Evidence level

Exploratory hypothesis. Upgrade requires independent PatientIDs, cell-type-matched lung/uterus references, and ATAC or spatial localization.

## Testable predictions

1. The lung-adaptation program should recur in independent pulmonary LAM donors rather than being driven by LAM3/LAM4;
2. its core genes should localize with ECM, lymphatic or hypoxic niches in independent spatial data;
3. lineage genes should be retained across tissues, whereas the lung-adaptation-specific component should be weaker in the uterine lineage reference.
