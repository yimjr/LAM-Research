# Known-program positive-control benchmark (extended)

Top-gene Jaccard and expression benchmarking provide different information. CORE2 had zero top-50 Jaccard recovery in the original pass, but the extended analysis shows positive donor-level expression enrichment in LAM1–LAM4 and NMF loading associations. Top-gene lists should therefore not be used alone to decide whether CORE2 was recovered.

The currently usable benchmark categories are CORE1, CORE2, CORE3 identity, LAM myogenic/contractile and ECM remodeling. SLS, IS and MDK treatment-associated programs have uneven expression or loading recovery and cannot yet support novelty claims for unknown treatment states.

TF/regulon/pathway layers have not yet been run in the current runtime, so final novelty assessment remains gated. CORE/ECM candidate analysis can continue, while treatment-associated unknown programs remain gated.
